GIPS® Standards Compliance Statement

In deep respect for the CFA Institute and the GIPS® Standards.

Dear Reader,

In deep respect for the CFA Institute and its GIPS Standards, the Fund is fully committed to absolute calculation rigor and performance transparency. The Fund applies GIPS-aligned methodologies for performance measurement and risk analytics, including exact time-weighted compounding, annualized risk-adjusted ratios, and fair-value portfolio valuation.

Operating as a private family-owned fund, we do not pursue formal third-party GIPS verification, yet every figure is computed to mirror international institutional standards. This framework ensures that our historical returns and risk-adjusted metrics remain entirely unskewed, offering investors a reliable, objective foundation to evaluate our long-term strategy.
