Our GIPS® Standards Statement

Out of the highest regard for the CFA Institute and its GIPS Standards.

Dear Reader,

In deep respect for the CFA Institute and its Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS), the Fund is fully committed to calculation rigor and performance transparency. For internal management and reference purposes only, we voluntarily utilize select GIPS methodologies as our technical guidelines for performance measurement and risk analytics, incorporating time-weighted compounding, annualized risk-adjusted ratios, and fair-value portfolio valuation.

Operating strictly as a private family-owned fund, we do not pursue or maintain formal GIPS compliance or third-party verification. Instead, this framework serves purely as an internal pedagogical tool to ensure our historical returns and risk metrics are evaluated with high institutional discipline, offering an objective foundation for our long-term strategy.
